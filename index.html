Hi! I'm Hasan: Product & Delivery Manager, Game Producer, Product Strategist

I'm a Game Product & Delivery Manager with 10+ years of experience working across mobile, PC, and live-service platforms. Throughout my career, I've contributed to a diverse range of projects, including mobile RPGs, 4X strategy games, PC MMORPGs, hybrid-casual and puzzle games, interactive livestreaming platforms, and social applications.

My experience spans the entire product lifecycle, from initial concept and roadmap planning to development, testing, release, and live operations. I've worked closely with engineering, design, publishing, and external stakeholders, taking ownership of product roadmaps, backlog management, sprint planning, technical documentation, UI/UX, and release management. I've also served as a final QA gatekeeper across multiple projects, ensuring features, builds, and releases meet quality and market-readiness standards.

Over the years, I've worked on several notable projects, including Action Anime, a monster-collection RPG with live operations and regional localization; Taqki, an interactive livestreaming platform that scaled to over 5 million monthly audience-hours; Muluk Al Naar, a B2B 4X strategy game; and Nifty Craft, a PC MMORPG combining PvPvE deckbuilding combat with player economies.

Most recently, I've been working as a Producer at Symmetric Studio, overseeing the development and delivery of a portfolio of hybrid-casual and casual mobile games. Working with a five-person team, I introduced Agile/Scrum workflows, established production and release processes, and coordinated development across engineering, design, and multimedia. My responsibilities also include product planning, feature prioritization, QA sign-off, UI/UX direction, game systems and economy balancing, and implementing analytics and advertising monetization infrastructure. During this time, we successfully shipped four titles within 10 months.

Beyond these projects, I've contributed to a variety of mobile applications and games across puzzle, party, card, quiz, dating, short-form video, and RPG genres. This breadth of experience has given me a strong understanding of different audiences, platforms, business models, and development challenges.

I enjoy working at the intersection of product, technology, and game development: turning ideas into actionable plans, helping teams overcome challenges, identifying opportunities for improvement, and making sure products get across the finish line.

If you're interested in collaborating, discussing a project, or exploring opportunities, feel free to reach out!
