Product-minded. Delivery-focused.

I'm a Product Manager with 10+ years of experience taking digital products from ideas and requirements through development, release, and ongoing improvement. My background spans mobile games, PC MMORPGs, interactive platforms, and B2B software — different products, but a consistent focus on understanding what needs to be built and helping teams deliver it.

I work across the practical parts of product management: clarifying business goals, shaping priorities, translating requirements into developer-ready work, coordinating cross-functional teams, managing scope and delivery risks, and validating what gets shipped. I'm comfortable working closely with engineering and design, as well as with leadership, clients, and external partners.

My experience is hands-on. I've helped introduce team workflows, owned backlogs and release readiness, supported product decisions with user feedback and performance data, and taken responsibility for final testing when quality mattered. At Symmetric Studio, for example, I coordinated a five-person team across four casual and hybrid-casual titles, introduced sprint routines, and helped improve Day 1 retention from 10% to 28% through early-game funnel analysis and product fixes.

Earlier work includes helping validate Nifty Craft through streamer campaigns, Steam Next Fest showcases, and external playtests that contributed to 14,000 wishlists; and supporting Taqki, an interactive livestreaming platform that reached more than 5 million monthly audience-hours.

I bring a product manager's focus on outcomes together with a delivery manager's attention to execution. I don't claim to be the deepest specialist in every discipline — my strength is connecting the problem, the people, and the work needed to move a product forward.
